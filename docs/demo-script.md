AI for Housing Hackathon, Track 1. We're [names]; this is ByRight PGH. The URA board has asked City real-estate staff for a Hazelwood housing push. Which City lots can take a small home, through which channel, and what does each home cost the public? Today that is a week of spreadsheets and zoning lookups. Citywide, 3,641 of 11,338 vacant City lots pass the use-table and lot-size screen. Nine also clear the cost-and-return screen and are for sale.

Hazelwood has 797 City records. 754 are in encoded districts; 285 pass the use table and lot-size standards. 123 are for sale with no slope, mine or flood flag, and 106 are at least a thousand square feet. Those 106 are candidates for staff review: 98 through URA Transfer, eight through Public Sale. None clears the cost screen at market.

Ten projects need about 2.59 million dollars of gap at $185 a foot, $259,000 each. At $150 it is 1.97 million; at $215, 3.13 million. That is a screen, not a subsidy award.

The first candidate, 4623 Chatsworth: a house is allowed by the use table in R1D-H, and the lot clears the 1,200-square-foot minimum. Four of six checks pass, finance fails, and fit is not checked. Setbacks and height are not modeled, one parking space must fit, and hazards were tested at one point. Unknown never counts as a pass.

Land is the County's $400 assessment, construction $222,000, fees $77,700, value $71,213: short $258,897. Move construction to $215 and everything recomputes: $312,357 here, 3.13 million for ten, and the nine citywide drop to zero. The zoning result does not move.

Two lots lookup tools get wrong. 0 Forbes is LNC, with no minimum lot size. It is 259 square feet, and FAR caps the building at 518. Relief needed, and no money line. 5724 Murray Hill: the inventory says RM-M, the City map says R1D-L. We do not pick one; use stays unknown until someone confirms.

One of the nine that clears: 126 Carrington, Central Northside, Public Sale. A townhouse costs $350,450 against $391,048 of value, $5,553 above a ten percent return. At $195 a foot it is $15,237 short. Thin, and we say thin. Switch to a duplex: blocked. Back: every section follows. The File tab pre-fills the City's purchase request for that same townhouse. The applicant files; we do not.

Staff leave with a 797-row, 63-column CSV, every check stated, plus a one-page brief for the board packet.

Claude extracted the zoning tables from saved code captures. No model sits in the decision path: every verdict, count and dollar figure is tables, public data and arithmetic. Pilot: six weeks with the Land Bank and URA on Hazelwood and Larimer; City Planning checks 30 verdicts and we publish every disagreement. Decision support, not zoning advice.
